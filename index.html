Project Overview

Netflix Titles dataset analyzed with SQL and presented through an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Netflix's content catalog and answer practical business questions related to movies, TV shows, ratings, countries, genres, directors, actors and release trends.


The project demonstrates SQL data exploration, aggregation, filtering, string manipulation, CTEs, window functions and analytical thinking.

Key Insights

Important observations generated from the uploaded dataset.

🎬

Movies Dominate

Movies account for 6,131 titles, while TV Shows account for 2,676 titles in the dataset.

🌎

United States Leads

The United States is associated with 3,690 titles, making it the largest country entry in the dataset.

🇮🇳

Strong India Presence

India is associated with 1,046 Netflix titles in the dataset.

🎭

International Movies

International Movies is the most frequent genre/category with 2,752 associated titles.

📈

Major Growth Period

The dataset shows a strong increase in release-year records during the 2015–2020 period.

🎥

Missing Director Data

2,634 records have missing director information, highlighting a notable data-quality consideration.
